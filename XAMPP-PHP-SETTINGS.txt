TRACEGRAD BULK GALLERY - XAMPP PHP SETTINGS
============================================

Because PHP limits the number and total size of uploaded files, bulk/folder
uploads may need these settings.

Open the XAMPP Control Panel.
Beside Apache click Config > PHP (php.ini).

Find these settings and use values similar to:

upload_max_filesize = 10M
post_max_size = 256M
max_file_uploads = 100
max_execution_time = 300
max_input_time = 300
memory_limit = 256M

IMPORTANT:
- upload_max_filesize is PER FILE.
- post_max_size is the TOTAL HTTP upload request.
- max_file_uploads controls how many files PHP accepts in one request.
- After changing php.ini, SAVE it and RESTART APACHE.

TRACEGRAD's application-level limits in gallery-upload-handler.php are:
- Max 100 files per request.
- Max 10 MB per file.
- Max approximately 250 MB combined.

For ordinary use, smaller batches such as 20-50 photos at a time are more
reliable on local XAMPP.
